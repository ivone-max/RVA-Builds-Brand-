01 · About RVA Builds

Build a career in the trades, before you graduate.

RVA Builds connects Richmond Public Schools students with paid, hands-on careers in construction and the skilled trades, before they even graduate. It's a Richmond Ed Fund initiative, backed by Bloomberg Philanthropies and powered by city, union, and employer partners — moving students into Registered Apprenticeships in electrical, HVAC, welding, and construction. BuildWithin is the program's technology and apprenticeship-design partner.

Icon / app mark

The "VA" cube standalone — favicons, app icons, avatars, tight spaces.

Endorsement

RVA Builds is a program of the Richmond Ed Fund. Pair the marks in a lock-up or footer where the partnership needs to be explicit.

Richmond Ed Fund (parent) · white, use on darkrichmond-ed-fund-logo.png

Do

  • Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the cube icon.
  • Use full color on white or photography with a dark overlay.
  • Use the white mono mark on coral, purple, blue, or busy photos.
  • Scale proportionally — never stretch.

Don't

  • Don't recolor the cube or crane outside the brand palette.
  • Don't place the full-color mark on low-contrast backgrounds.
  • Don't add shadows, outlines, or effects to the mark.
  • Don't rotate, skew, or crowd the logo.

05 · Tone & voice

How RVA Builds sounds

Hopeful, trustworthy, civic, warm, data-informed, editorial. Full detail — writing rules, do/don't, sample copy — lives in docs/tone-and-voice.md.

Hopeful

"A choice-filled life is the standard."
Not fear-first ("kids fall through the cracks")

Trustworthy

"500 students, 17 partners, by 2028–29."
Not vague superlatives

Civic

"Right here in Richmond, Virginia."
Not generic, placeless copy

Warm

"A student earning a paycheck and a future."
Not "human capital" or "the pipeline" as subject

Data-informed

"$23/hour minimum after graduation."
Not rounded-up or unsourced stats

Editorial

Full sentences, active voice, real quotes.
Not jargon strings or exclamation points
Audience-specific voice

The candidate card below is verbatim from the live "For Students" page on rvabuilds.us — use it as the calibration point for tone. Employer and press examples are illustrative direction in the same voice.

To candidates · live copy
"Get paid to learn a career you'll keep." — RVA Builds is open to Richmond Public Schools students. Earn paid hours in the skilled trades and graduate straight into a real apprenticeship, at no cost to you.
To employers · illustrative
"Train your next hire while they're still in school. RVA Builds connects you with RPS students ready for paid, on-the-job experience — and a clear path to a Registered Apprenticeship."
To press · illustrative
"Backed by Bloomberg Philanthropies, RVA Builds is Richmond Ed Fund's citywide push to connect RPS students with paid apprenticeship pathways in the skilled trades."
