01 · About RVA Builds

Build a career in the trades, before you graduate.

RVA Builds connects Richmond Public Schools students with paid, hands-on careers in construction and the skilled trades, before they even graduate. A Richmond Ed Fund initiative, backed by Bloomberg Philanthropies, moving students into Registered Apprenticeships in electrical, HVAC, welding, and construction.

By the numbers
150
RPS grads aimed at registered apprenticeships in 3 years
500
Students in paid trades learning by 2028–29 (goal)
17
Partners: schools, the city, business & unions
120+
Paid work-based learning hours per student
$8M Bloomberg Philanthropies investment
$23/hr minimum total comp. after graduation

02 · Logo

Icon, endorsement & usage rules

Icon / app mark
Richmond Ed Fund endorsement · white, on dark

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the cube icon.
  • Use full color on white or photography with a dark overlay.
  • Scale proportionally — never stretch.

Don't

  • Don't recolor the cube or crane outside the brand palette.
  • Don't add shadows, outlines, or effects to the mark.
  • Don't rotate, skew, or crowd the logo.

05 · Tone & voice

How RVA Builds sounds

Hopeful, trustworthy, civic, warm, data-informed, editorial.

Hopeful

"A choice-filled life is the standard."
Not fear-first

Trustworthy

"500 students, 17 partners, by 2028–29."
Not vague superlatives

Civic

"Right here in Richmond, Virginia."
Not placeless copy

Warm

"A student earning a paycheck and a future."
Not "human capital"

Data-informed

"$23/hour minimum after graduation."
Not unsourced stats

Editorial

Full sentences, active voice, real quotes.
Not jargon strings
